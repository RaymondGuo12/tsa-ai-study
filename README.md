# AI Study Lab

A free study app for general TSA Artificial Intelligence preparation.

## Study features

- Study notes and a five-session plan
- 22 flashcards with self-ratings
- 20-question practice quiz with explanations and missed-question retries
- Prompting practice lab
- Progress reports you can download or print

Open `index.html` to study locally. Progress is saved in your browser on your device. No account or AI API key is required. The app does not call an AI service.

Independent study aid, not an official TSA test guide. Consult your advisor's full event guide for exact requirements.

References: [TSA event overview](https://tsaweb.org/competitions/high-school) and [NIST AI risk framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework).
